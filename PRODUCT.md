# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Principals and operations leaders at independent RIAs, wealth management firms, and multi-family offices. They are skeptical of tech hype, time-constrained, and deeply resistant to vendors who don't understand how their firms actually run. They read proposals carefully and spot vague claims immediately. They are the decision-maker and the budget-holder simultaneously.

## Product Purpose

Core Consulting builds and deploys AI systems for wealth management firms. The site exists to convert a skeptical prospect into a booked consultation: it must earn credibility fast, show concrete proof (real clients, real numbers), and make the path to engagement feel low-risk. Success is a submitted contact form from a qualified firm.

## Brand Personality

Bold, direct, results-first. The tone is that of a senior practitioner who has done this before, not a vendor pitching a solution. No hedging, no jargon, no hype. Every claim is backed by a number or a named outcome.

## Anti-references

- Big-4 consulting aesthetic: McKinsey navy, dense grid layouts, corporate stiffness, "thought leadership" vocabulary, stock-photo-of-a-handshake energy
- Crypto / fintech hype: dark-mode neons, "AI-powered everything", vague superlatives, speculative tone, dashboard screenshots of nothing
- Generic SaaS startup: Notion-beige, Inter-on-white, gradient CTAs, "transform your workflow" copy

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over promise.** Every marketing claim needs a number, a firm name, or a concrete outcome next to it. Assertions without evidence are noise.
2. **The surface earns the room.** The visual register should feel like the materials a credible boutique firm puts in front of a client, not a VC pitch deck.
3. **Restraint as authority.** Visual richness comes from confident typography and precise spacing, not decoration. Busy equals uncertain.
4. **Directness in every word.** No intro sentences that restate the heading. No buzzwords. Verb + object on every CTA.
5. **Motion that respects attention.** Animations reveal; they never distract. A wealth manager reading a proposal doesn't have time for flourish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Reduced motion honored globally. Color never the sole information carrier.
